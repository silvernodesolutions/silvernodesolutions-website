Privacy Policy

Silvernode Solutions

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Silvernode Solutions (“Silvernode,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, request an assessment or consultation, or use services and automation solutions we provide.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact and business information: name, business name, email address, telephone number, job title, business address, website, and information about your business.
  • Messages and inquiry details: information you include in website forms, email, WhatsApp conversations, consultation requests, support requests, or business assessments.
  • Service and workflow information: information you or your organization choose to connect to an automation, such as booking details, customer inquiries, order information, or records in connected business systems.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser, log, and usage information that may be collected by our website host, security tools, or service providers.

Please do not send us sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a service and we have agreed on appropriate handling arrangements.

2. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • respond to inquiries and provide requested assessments, consultations, demonstrations, and support;
  • prepare proposals, configure, operate, and maintain websites, chatbots, integrations, and business automation workflows;
  • manage appointments, customer requests, notifications, and records where you have asked us to do so;
  • communicate service updates, troubleshoot issues, protect our systems, and prevent misuse;
  • meet legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

3. Connected services and Google user data

Our services may connect with third-party platforms such as Google Sheets, Google Calendar, and Gmail, as well as messaging, hosting, database, automation, and AI service providers. The specific platforms depend on the service and integrations selected by you or your organization.

When you authorize a Google connection, we access only the Google data and permissions needed for the enabled functionality. For example, a workflow may use Google Sheets to store submitted assessment details, Google Calendar to manage appointments, or Gmail to send requested notifications.

We use Google user data to provide or improve the user-facing features you have requested. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models. We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide the requested service, comply with law, or protect against security or abuse issues. Any service provider receiving such data must be authorized to process it for the relevant service and subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. When we share information

We may share information with service providers that help us host, secure, communicate, store data, or operate requested workflows. We may also share information with platforms you direct us to connect, when needed to perform the service; with professional advisers; or where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and security. We do not sell personal information.

5. WhatsApp and other communications

If you contact us through WhatsApp, your messages and contact details may be processed by WhatsApp/Meta and by the systems we use to receive and respond to those messages. Do not send confidential or sensitive information through a messaging channel unless you are comfortable with that channel and it is appropriate for the purpose.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and protect our systems. Retention periods depend on the type of information, the service, and any agreement with the customer. Where we operate workflows on behalf of a client, the client may control the retention of information in the systems and accounts they own.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No website, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your own accounts and access credentials, and for deciding which information to submit or connect.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also withdraw an authorization for a connected third-party account through that platform's account settings. To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. 9. International processing

Silvernode may work with customers and service providers in different countries. Your information may therefore be processed in a country other than the country where you live, subject to applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards where required.

10. Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can review the request.
